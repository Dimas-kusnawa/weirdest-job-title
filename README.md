A program that writes one job title to `/output/entry.txt`: "Senior Apology Writer for the Office Printer" (44 characters, inside the 60-character cap.) A real-sounding corporate role with one hilariously specific twist — a senior writer whose entire job is apologizing to the office printer, the machine everyone hates and nobody can fix. It lands in one read.
